## 1. Underwater adventuring fundamentals

### 1.2 Turn cost and Fishy

**Underwater adventures cost 2 adventures each unless the Fishy effect is active.** The extra
turn is charged after combat resolves. This halves-or-doubles the entire budget: a 10-combat
pearl at cap costs 20 adventures without Fishy, 10 with it.

Fishy sources (turns of Fishy): **fishy pipe 10 (1/day)**, fish juice box 20, concentrated
fish broth 30, cuppa Gill tea 30, powdered candy sushi set 30, sushi (Nigiri 20 / Maki 45 /
dragonfish-caviar Maki 50 / Bento 80), Aldebaran sardines 60 (2 full), shoo-fish pie 20
(2 full), Centauri fish wine 60 (2 drunk), fishy paste 10 (4 spleen), Sea jelly 10 (1 spleen),
Fish sauce 30 (1 spleen), Lutz the Ice Skate 30, The Haggling (Lucky) 20, Just the Facts 10,
various 5-turn fish meats. Warm Belly adds +10 turns when eating Fishy-granting Sea food.
(No such thing as "Fishy Fortifier" — the name doesn't exist on the wiki.)

### 1.4 Underwater combat rules

- All underwater monsters are **CLEESH-immune**.
- Sea drops are **pickpocket-immune** and **cannot be yellow-rayed** (exceptions: bazookafish
  bubble gum, Alewife™ Ale, Dive Bar booze slugs; pulled yellow taffy does force drops;
  Using the Force works on most Sea items; Feel Envy does not work underwater).
- No adventuring in slimeform.

## 2. Unblemished pearl mechanics

Item #11976, autosell **11,037 meat** (path-number pun); appears tradeable (no restriction
tag; unpullable in the 11,037 Leagues path softcore). Five pearls confront the **Nautical
Seaceress** in the **11,037 Leagues Under the Sea** path (requirement added ~Sep 1, 2025).

- Post-combat progress: **1.7% × floor(elemental resistance ÷ 3)**, min 1.7%, **max 10% at
  18 resistance** (the cap binds: 6 × 1.7 = 10.2). At cap: **10 combats minimum per pearl**.
- **One pearl per zone per day; progress does NOT survive rollover.** Negative resistance
  (e.g. The Colors...) gives negative progress.
- Zone → element: Anemone Mine **Spooky** · The Dive Bar **Sleaze** · Madness Reef **Stench**
  · The Marinara Trench **Hot** · The Briniest Deepests **Cold**.
- Resistance past 18 is wasted (for pearl purposes). Whether negative progress floors at 0 is
  UNKNOWN.

## 3. Zones

you). All monsters in all five zones are **fixed-stat (non-scaling)**, have **no physical
resistance**, and meat values are pre-pressure-penalty.

## 4. Cross-zone summary for pearlo

**Outfit targets per zone**: 18 of the zone's element resistance (cap; more is wasted for
pearls) + an air source + familiar breathing. Effect-based resistance tops out at **+8
all-element** (Egged On +3, Elemental Saucesphere +2, Feeling Peaceful +2, Astral Shell +1;
wiki-verified 2026-08-07) plus **+8 more for cold/sleaze only** (Scarysauce +2 +
Scariersauce +6, stacking — Scariersauce needs a velour viscometer wielded at cast time).
So gear/parka must supply ≥10 resistance in hot/stench/spooky zones but as little as ≥2 in
cold/sleaze zones with the full sauce stack — the Briniest Deepests (cold) is the easiest
cap, another reason it's the v1 zone. Prefer air sources without item/init penalties
(effects, Crown of Ed, Waterproofly). Moxie for full no-hit by zone: Mine 510 · Dive Bar
**610** · Reef 510 (but dragonfish elemental hits bypass moxie) · Trench 560 · Deepests 435.
Kill coverage: ~800 damage one-shots every pearl-zone monster (HP 500–800; all fixed-stat,
no physical resistance, CLEESH-immune).

**Mandatory macro branches**:

- acoustic electric eel (Deepests): **no melee attacks** — spell/skill kill.
- pufferfish (Reef): win initiative → **stun round 1** → burst (poison doubles per acted
  round, triggers hit-or-miss, kills through victory).
- magic dragonfish (Reef): kill fast; its The Colors... (−5 all res) reverses pearl
  progress; cure with Disco Power Nap if caught.
- Anemone combatant (Mine) & jamfish (Reef): can inflict Majorly Poisoned (−90%/−11 all
  attributes, 10 turns) — carry anti-anti-antidote / Disco Power Nap / Spew Poison.
- Anemone Mine: **avoid equipping Mer-kin digpick** (converts the zone to the mining
  mini-game — zero combats, zero pearl progress).

**Turn budget**: without Fishy every encounter costs 2 adventures — at the 10%-progress cap
that's ~20 adv/pearl, ~100 adv for all five zones; with Fishy ~10/pearl, ~50 total. Fishy
is the single biggest efficiency lever (fishy pipe 1/day, sushi, fish juice box, etc.).

**Choice automation**: set choice 306→1 (Mine, SC/TT skill), 307→1 / 308→1 (Dive Bar class
skills), 309→2 (Barback: leave, or 1 to collect seaodes ≤3/day), 310/311 (Reef scale trading
— free, no adventure), 302/303→1, 304/305 per resource plan (Trench).

**Meat/item expectations**: pressure crushes drops (−200% Mine/Bar/Trench, −100% Reef, −75%
Deepests); Sea drops are pickpocket/yellow-ray-immune. Don't build farming EV around drops
unless stacking diver gear toward 0%; the pearls are the payoff.

**Wiki-flagged unknowns**: combat rates for Mine/Bar/Trench; per-attack damage numbers;
eel counter floor ("89?"); flip-out exact stat math; whether negative pearl progress floors
at 0.

## 5. Resistance-support familiars

Four familiars matter for hitting the 18-resistance pearl cap (wiki pages fetched
2026-08-06). All four say "Cannot breathe underwater" on the wiki — per user-confirmed
mechanics that only means _no innate breathing_: each works underwater with an enabler.
The catch is **which enabler**: das boot (−10 fam weight) and little bitty bathysphere
(−20) occupy the familiar-equipment slot these familiars want for other things, so
**Driving Waterproofly (or Wet Willied) is the enabler of choice for all four**.

### Extra-equipment familiars ("holding" familiars)

- **Left-Hand Man** (#278, Mr. Store Apr 2020, hatchling: sinistral homunculus).
  "Allows you to equip an additional off-hand item" — the off-hand rides in its
  familiar-equipment slot. **User-confirmed (2026-08): it can wear generic familiar
  equipment (das boot etc.) instead, but then it holds no off-hand** — so underwater, use
  Waterproofly/Wet Willied to keep the extra off-hand slot. Cannot hold weapons (even with
  Double-Fisted Skull Smashing). Attacks every round for weight..weight+3.
  The wiki never explicitly states the held off-hand's enchantments transfer to the player,
  but that is its entire purpose (user-confirmed for resistance purposes; KoLmafia's
  maximizer models it).
- **Disembodied Hand** (#92, Mr. Store Oct 2008, hatchling: spooky rattling cigar box).
  Equips any **1-handed weapon** — excluding chefstaves, 1-handed accordions, and
  single-equip weapons — ignoring the player's stat requirements, and explicitly (wiki
  verbatim) "imbue[s] you with the enchantment of the weapon". Caveats: class-locked
  enchantments need the matching class; elemental/spell damage bonuses stack with (don't
  duplicate) the player's; the held weapon does not complete outfits. Attacks every round.
  Cannot sit in Buddy Bjorn/Crown. (The wiki is internally inconsistent on generic familiar
  equipment; user-confirmed it works — see the slot rule below.)

Grimoire integration (verified in `grimoire-kolmafia/dist/outfit.js`): `Outfit.equip()`'s
fallback chain (`equipNonAccessory → equipAccessory → equipUsingDualWield →
equipUsingFamiliar`) **automatically switches the outfit's familiar to Left-Hand Man /
Disembodied Hand** when asked for a second off-hand / extra weapon — it refuses "Single
Equip" items and only fires when the outfit has no familiar set and the famequip slot is
free. So `outfit.equip($item\`some resistance off-hand\`)` twice does the Left-Hand Man
dance itself — but it _consumes the familiar choice_.

### Weight-scaling resistance familiars

- **Exotic Parrot** (#72, Black Market, 500k meat). Grants +1 resistance level per 4 lbs,
  cycling **Hot → Cold → Spooky → Stench → Sleaze** — i.e. **+1 to a specific element per
  20 lbs**, with the element's first point at: Hot 1 lb, Cold 5, Spooky 9, Stench 13,
  Sleaze 17. No cap. Its `cracker` (+15 fam weight) lives in the famequip slot — with
  Waterproofly the parrot keeps the cracker underwater; with das boot it nets −25 lbs vs
  cracker (≥1 resistance level lost). Does not attack.
- **Mu** (#265, rare Pokéfam from Tall Grass). "Gives elemental resistances based on weight
  like Exotic Parrot" (the wiki gives no independent table). Also attacks with prismatic
  damage — weight/6..weight/3 _per element_, every round at ≥26 lbs — which is relevant
  underwater where all pearl-zone monsters have no elemental resistance. Its `luck incense`
  (+10 wt, **+25% item**) disappears at end of day. Can't bjorn/enthrone.

Slot rule for both hands — **user-confirmed (2026-08)**: they accept generic familiar
equipment normally, but the famequip slot is one slot — das boot _or_ the held
weapon/off-hand, never both. Underwater with breathing gear on the familiar, a holding
familiar contributes nothing extra; only Waterproofly/Wet Willied preserves their value.

## 6. Stunning (wiki `Stunning` + linked pages, fetched 2026-08-07)

Needed for the pufferfish (stun round 1 after winning initiative → poison never starts)
and the magic dragonfish (elemental attacks bypass Moxie; stun prevents The Colors...).

### Mechanics

Four attack-prevention categories:

- **Stagger** — blocks the current round only; 100% unless the monster has stagger
  immunity. Staggers take priority over stuns and **don't consume a stun charge** that
  round.
- **Stun** — blocks X rounds **starting with the current round**; stackable. Multiple
  stuns queue and are consumed in game-introduction order, not usage order.
- **Quick-cast** — skill doesn't advance the round at all (no passives, no familiar act,
  no attack).
- **Block** — negates only the damage; monster passives still fire. Blocks keep working
  above +151 ML where stuns/staggers fail. Notable for pearlo: **Blood Bubble** (Sauceror
  skill, Booke of Vampyric Knowledge, permable) — **costs 30 HP, not MP**, 3 turns of
  "the first hit per combat auto-misses" (multi-attack monsters can still land extras).
  Blood Bond likewise costs HP. Stacks with the parka stagger as a second free layer.

A stunned monster skips _most but not all_ special actions; whether a stun suppresses
already-started pufferfish poison ticks is **[NOT ON WIKI]** — the confirmed play is to
prevent the first tick entirely (stun before it ever acts).

**Stun resistance scales with BONUS Monster Level, not base stats**: starting at +51
bonus ML, monsters shrug stuns at (bonus ML − 50)% per round; +151 bonus ML = stagger
immunity. The pearl monsters' base attack (375–600) does **not** trigger this — it's
your +ML that does. **pearlo should run zero +ML** (no reason to add any, and it would
break stun reliability).

### Recommended combat-control stack

**Primary plan — the round-1 one-shot (user decision, 2026-08).** One non-melee action
dealing **≥800 damage** on round 1 circumvents every special behavior in all five zones:
pufferfish poison triggers only "the first round in which the pufferfish attacks" — dead
fish never attack; the dragonfish never lands its moxie-bypassing hits or The Colors...;

physical or elemental resistance (HP tops out at 800: ganger, giant squid). The

the monster's first attack, so the player's round-1 action still comes first. (In Madness

2. **Retro cape (Heck General)** auto-3-round-stun when the back slot isn't needed for air.

## 7. Deleveling (wiki `Deleveling` + stat pages, fetched 2026-08-07)

Deleveling weakens the monster without touching stat gains. Why it matters given the
formulas (from `Hit_Chance`/`Monsters`):

- Player hit chance: `(6 + attack − monsterDefense)/11`, clamped — **each point of Defense
  removed ≈ +9.1% hit chance**. Relevant vs lounge lizardfish (Def 630) and Anemone
  combatant (Def 585) for weapon-based plans.
- Monster damage: `(Atk − Mox, min 0) + 20–25% of Atk − DR` — Attack deleveling shrinks
  both terms (note the 20–25%-of-Attack chip damage exists even when fully outmoxied;
  vs the tippler's 600 Attack that's ~120–150 per landed hit).
- Monster hit chance ≈ `55 + (Atk − Mox) × 5.5` %.

Key caveats (wiki-stated):

- **Deleveling does NOT fix stun resistance** — that keys off _bonus ML_ only. It also
  doesn't change stat gains (unlike ±ML).
- Percentage-vs-flat stacking order, universal floors, and caps are all **unstated**
  (only Curse of Weaksauce documents "attack not below 1, defense unbounded below").
- Briniest flip-out doubling "is not scaled by percentage deleveling" (zone page).
- ⚠ **Mer-kin weaksauce is NOT a deleveler** despite the name — it's a sushi ingredient
  (bento box). The similarly-named deleveler is **Mer-kin mouthsoap** (−250 to −300,
  staggers, works on Mer-kin — tippler, miner, diver are phylum mer-kin).

### Fit with the combat plan

Deleveling is the third layer: (1) round-1 one-shot kills before specials fire; (2) parka
stagger/stun covers the cast; (3) **deleveling makes the fallback rounds safe** — cheaper
no-hit thresholds (deleveled Atk vs the tippler's 610 bar), less chip damage, and better
weapon hit chance where spells aren't available. The Space Jellyfish is a standout: innate
water-breather, 100% start-of-combat delevel, plus its Trench/eel utilities — a strong
default when no resistance/holding familiar is needed. (Magic Dragonfish's weight-scaled
spell% was considered for this role and ruled out — not impactful enough in practice;
user decision 2026-08-07.)

### Choosing for pearlo

Only one familiar slot: the trade-off is **an extra enchantment slot** (hands — worth up to
whatever the best res off-hand/weapon gives, typically +3-5 toward the zone element) vs
**weight-scaled all-element res + prismatic damage** (parrot/Mu — a 40-lb parrot is only
+2-3 to a given element) vs **drop/meat familiars** (pearls don't need drops, so res wins
while below the 18 cap). Once 18 res is reachable from gear/effects alone, the familiar
slot is free for anything that breathes. The maximizer objective should include the
familiar's contribution automatically when the familiar is set before maximizing.
